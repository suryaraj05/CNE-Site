Limits of liability for cargo claims

Cargo liability limits refer to the maximum amount of insurance cover (that is, the amount of money) that an insurer will provide for the loss of or damage to goods being transported.

By sea - Hague-Visby Rules

  • £2.12 per kg (SDR 2.00 per kg)
  • £706.70 per package (SDR 666.67 per package)

By road - CMR

  • £8.83 per kg (SDR 8.33 per kg)

By air - Montreal Convention

  • £23.32 per kg (SDR 22.00 per kg)

By air - Warsaw Convention

  • £18.02 per kg (SDR 17.00 per kg)

By STC - BIFA

  • £2.12 per kg (SDR 2.00 per kg)

Sterling amounts are based on the value of 1 SDR, which stood at £1.06004 according to the International Monetary Fund website on 13 December 2023.

Abbreviations

  • CMR: Carriage of Goods by Road Act
  • STC: Standard Trading Conditions
  • SDR: Special Drawing Rights
