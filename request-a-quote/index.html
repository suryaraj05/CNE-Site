Frequently asked

Quote questions

Is the quote free?

Yes. Every quote is free and comes with no obligation to book.

What information do you need for an accurate quote?

The departure and delivery cities, the weight and dimensions of your goods, a description of what you’re shipping and when it will be ready. If customs clearance is involved, having your commercial invoice and EORI number to hand also helps.

Can I request more than one service?

Yes. Choose the main service in the form and mention anything else - such as customs clearance alongside freight - in the special information box.

I’m not sure which transport mode I need. Can you advise?

Of course. Choose “Not sure - please advise” and we’ll recommend the best balance of cost and speed for your shipment.
