Frequently asked

Global services questions

Which countries can you ship to and from?

We move freight worldwide by air and sea, with particular expertise in China to UK imports, UK to USA air freight, and baggage to China and India.

Do you handle customs clearance as part of international shipping?

Yes. We submit UK import and export declarations to HMRC through CDS and manage the documentation needed at destination, so customs is handled by the same team as your freight.

Should I choose air or sea freight?

Air freight is best for urgent, lightweight or high-value goods. Sea freight is the economical choice for larger or less time-sensitive cargo. We’ll recommend the right option for your shipment.

Can you collect from and deliver to my door?

Yes. Most of our global services are available door to door, including collection, customs clearance at both ends and final delivery.
