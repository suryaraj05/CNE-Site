Introduction

A local team with global reach

Cargo Northeast Ltd specialises in customs clearance and logistics for businesses in Newcastle and North East England. From our office at Freight Village, Newcastle Airport, we arrange sea freight, air freight and road transport, and serve the region’s key ports - including the Port of Tyne, Sunderland and Teesport.

Our personalised logistics solutions are designed to give reliable, efficient service to businesses trading locally and internationally. With a network of trusted partners, we’re your go-to provider for logistics in North East England and beyond - and a second office in Mumbai supports customers shipping between the UK and India.

Customs clearance

Customs clearance in the UK and North East England

We provide customs clearance in Newcastle, across North East England and throughout the UK, making sure every shipment meets regulatory requirements so you avoid unnecessary delays and costs.

Our experience at the Port of Tyne, Sunderland and Teesport means we can clear goods quickly for businesses in the region. We prepare the documentation and liaise directly with the customs authorities, so your imports and exports keep moving.

  • Import & export declarations
  • HMRC & CDS submissions
  • Documentation prepared for you
  • Port of Tyne, Sunderland & Teesport

Our holistic approach

Logistics is more than moving goods

At Cargo Northeast, logistics is about offering a complete solution. From customs clearance to final delivery, every part of your shipment’s journey is handled with precision and care.

By combining local expertise in North East England with international freight capability, we tailor our services to the specific needs of each client - so businesses across Newcastle and the UK can rely on us for seamless, dependable service.

Beyond conventional logistics

When you choose Cargo Northeast, you gain more than a logistics provider: you gain a partner committed to helping your business succeed. With access to key ports and transport hubs across North East England, we offer a strategic advantage for local and international trade.

From customs clearance in the UK to managing global freight, we simplify the logistics process. Let us take care of your logistics so you can focus on driving your business forward.
