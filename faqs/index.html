Customs clearance

Customs and compliance

What customs clearance services do you provide?

We prepare and submit import and export declarations to HMRC through the Customs Declaration Service (CDS). That includes confirming commodity codes, calculating duty and import VAT, and applying postponed VAT accounting where it is available to you.

Do I need an EORI number to import or export?

Businesses moving goods into or out of Great Britain generally need a GB EORI number. If you don’t have one yet, we can guide you through the application before your goods move.

What documents do I need for customs clearance?

Usually a commercial invoice, a packing list and the transport document (air waybill, bill of lading or CMR), plus your EORI number. Some goods also need licences or certificates - we check your paperwork before we file the declaration.

Can you clear goods arriving at any UK airport or port?

Yes. Declarations are submitted electronically through CDS, so we can clear shipments arriving at Heathrow, Manchester, East Midlands, Newcastle and other UK airports and seaports.

What is postponed VAT accounting?

Postponed VAT accounting lets VAT-registered importers account for import VAT on their VAT return instead of paying it when the goods arrive, which helps cash flow. We can tell you whether it applies to your shipment and declare it correctly.

Do you handle customs for goods moving between the UK and the EU?

Yes. Since Brexit, goods moving between Great Britain and the EU need customs declarations. We handle the paperwork alongside our UK–EU road transport, so the freight and the customs are managed by one team.

Freight & shipping

Air, sea and road freight

Which freight services do you offer?

We arrange air freight for time-sensitive cargo, FCL and LCL sea freight for larger or less urgent consignments, and road transport between the UK and the EU - each with customs clearance and onward delivery included where needed.

Which countries do you ship to and from?

We regularly handle freight from China and India to the UK, air freight between the UK and the USA, and shipments from the UK to the rest of the world. Tell us your origin and destination and we’ll confirm the best route.

Can you help Amazon sellers with FBA shipments?

Yes. We support Amazon sellers with preparation, labelling and delivery into Amazon fulfilment centres, as well as the import customs clearance for stock coming from overseas.

How do I get a freight quote?

Use our quote request form, call us or email the team. Include the origin and destination, the number of packages with weights and dimensions, and a description of the goods, and we’ll come back with a clear, no-obligation quote.

How can I track my shipment?

You can follow your consignment on our Track Your Shipment page, and our team keeps you updated at each stage - from collection and customs clearance through to final delivery.

What terms do you trade under?

We are a BIFA member and trade under BIFA standard trading conditions. Full details are set out in our terms and conditions.

Baggage, moves & storage

Baggage, house moves and storage

Can you send personal baggage from the UK to India or China?

Yes. We offer door-to-door delivery of personal baggage and parcels from the UK to India, and a baggage delivery service to China - a practical alternative to paying excess baggage charges on your flight.

Does personal baggage need customs paperwork?

Yes. Personal effects still have to be cleared through customs at the destination. We’ll tell you which documents and packing details are needed before your baggage is collected.

Where do you offer house moves?

We provide affordable house moves across Newcastle, Tyne and Wear and Durham, with prices starting from £49.

Do you offer storage?

Yes. We offer secure, flexible storage in Newcastle from £50 per month for students, residents and businesses, with free collection and delivery.
