Introduction

Comprehensive logistics in Scotland and the UK

Cargo Northeast Ltd specialises in comprehensive logistics solutions in Scotland and the UK, giving businesses efficient, cost-effective service. From navigating the complexities of customs clearance to timely delivery by road, air and sea freight, we handle every step with precision and care.

Operating from key locations in the North East and Scotland - including the Port of Tyne, Teesport and Sunderland, alongside other major transport hubs - we’re equipped for both local and international logistics. Frequently asked

Questions about our services

What services does Cargo Northeast offer?

Customs clearance, air, sea and road freight, China to UK imports, UK to USA and worldwide shipping, Amazon FBA logistics, baggage delivery to India and China, house moves and secure storage.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re based at Freight Village, Newcastle Airport, and serve the North East, Scotland and the whole of the UK, with road freight to and from the EU and air and sea freight worldwide.

Can you clear customs for goods I’ve shipped with another carrier?

Yes. Customs declarations are submitted electronically to HMRC through CDS, so we can clear goods arriving at any UK port or airport, whoever moved the freight.

How do I get a quote?

Send your shipment details through our quote form or call our team. We’ll come back with a clear, no-obligation quote.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us what you’re moving and where, and we’ll recommend the right solution with a clear, no-obligation quote.
