Post-Brexit customs

Customs, handled properly

Since Brexit, almost every shipment into or out of the UK needs a customs declaration submitted to HMRC through the Customs Declaration Service (CDS). Getting the detail right keeps your cargo moving and avoids costly holds.

We prepare and submit declarations for you, confirm the right commodity codes, and make sure duty and import VAT are accounted for correctly - including postponed VAT accounting where it applies.

  • HMRC & CDS declarations
  • EORI number guidance
  • Commodity code classification
  • Duty & import VAT
  • Postponed VAT accounting

Frequently asked

Customs and freight questions

Where is Cargo Northeast based?

Our UK office is at Freight Village, Newcastle Airport (NE13 8BH). We serve customers across North East England, Scotland and the whole of the UK, with a second office in Mumbai, India.

Can you clear goods arriving at any UK airport or port?

Yes. Customs declarations are submitted electronically to HMRC through CDS, so we can clear shipments arriving at Heathrow, Manchester, East Midlands and other UK airports and seaports.

Do I need an EORI number to import or export?

Businesses moving goods into or out of Great Britain generally need a GB EORI number. If you don’t have one yet, we can guide you through applying before your goods move.

Do you handle both imports and exports?

Yes. We handle import and export declarations, freight booking and onward delivery, so your shipment is managed end to end by one team.
