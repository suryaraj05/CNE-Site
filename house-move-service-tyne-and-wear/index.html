Frequently asked

House move questions

How much does a house move cost?

Local moves within North East England start from £49, and UK-wide relocations from £99. The final price depends on the weight and size of your items and the distance travelled.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover Newcastle, Tyne and Wear, Durham, Sunderland, South Shields, Ashington, Teesport and the wider North East, and we move customers to destinations across the UK.

Do you offer packing and furniture assembly?

Yes. We provide packing and unpacking assistance as well as full disassembly and reassembly of large furniture.

Do you have a student moving service?

Yes. Students in Newcastle, Sunderland and Durham can use our free collection and same-day move service.

Can you store my belongings between moves?

Yes. We offer secure, affordable storage in Newcastle with collection and delivery, which can be combined with your move.
