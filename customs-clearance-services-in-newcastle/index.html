Customs at Newcastle Airport

Declarations done right, first time

Goods leaving or entering the UK through Newcastle need the correct customs formalities completed and declared to HMRC through the Customs Declaration Service (CDS). Accurate information and good communication are what keep shipments from being held.

We make sure your EORI number, declarations and duty and VAT details are accurate and compliant from the start, coordinate with the airport and freight providers, and handle any customs queries so you don’t have to.

  • Import & export declarations via CDS
  • EORI number checks
  • Duty & VAT guidance
  • Commodity & shipment data
  • Customs query handling

Frequently asked

Newcastle customs questions

Where are you based in Newcastle?

Our UK office is at Freight Village, Newcastle Airport, NE13 8BH. You can call +44 7354 595649 or email opsuk@cargonortheast.com.

Can you help with customs clearance for exports from Newcastle Airport?

Yes. We coordinate export declarations, shipment documentation and the freight and airport processes involved in exporting goods from Newcastle Airport.

What documents are usually needed?

Commonly a commercial invoice, a packing list, an air waybill and a customs declaration. Some shipments need extra paperwork such as licences or certificates of origin - we’ll advise on yours.

Do your Newcastle freight services cover urgent shipments?

Yes. Air freight suits urgent and high-value consignments, and we coordinate customs clearance alongside the freight to keep time-sensitive goods moving.
