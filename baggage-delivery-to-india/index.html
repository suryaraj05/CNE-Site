What you can send

Personal belongings and commercial goods

Whether you’re sending excess baggage, personal effects or business-related items, our team packs and handles your shipment carefully to minimise the risk of damage, with real-time tracking throughout.

Some items are restricted or prohibited for international shipping - for example flammable liquids, aerosols, compressed gases and loose lithium batteries. Ask us before you pack and we’ll confirm what can travel.

Frequently asked

Baggage to India questions

Do you collect baggage from my home?

Yes. We collect from your home, office or another preferred location anywhere in the UK.

Do you handle customs clearance in India?

Yes. We manage UK export formalities and Indian import clearance, so your baggage complies with both countries’ regulations.

Should I send my baggage by air or sea?

Air is faster and suits urgent or smaller shipments; sea is usually more economical for larger volumes. We’ll recommend the best option when you request a quote.

Which cities in India do you deliver to?

We deliver to any city in India, including Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Bangalore and Hyderabad.

Can I track my baggage?

Yes. You’ll receive updates throughout, and you can follow your shipment’s progress on our tracking page.
